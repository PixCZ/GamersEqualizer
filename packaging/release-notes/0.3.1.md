## Is your mic live?

A small release with one thing you will see: the tray icon now shows your microphone. While an app
records (Discord, a game, a recorder), the bars in the icon move with your voice, so you can tell
at a glance that your squad hears you, and that you are not talking into a muted channel.

- **The bars follow your voice.** They show the level others hear after the microphone effect, not
  the raw input.
- **It reacts when an app starts recording.** Before, the icon could take about a second and a half
  to notice. Now Windows tells the app and the icon changes at once.
- **Off if you do not like it.** **Settings**, "Show the microphone level in the tray icon". It
  needs microphone processing on, and Windows must show the icon on the taskbar: in the hidden
  icons the bars stand still.

Under the hood the background service now sleeps when nothing is going on (window closed, nothing
recording, no game): about 4 wakeups a second instead of about 12 on our test PC. We have not
measured battery life, so we do not promise any.

**Also:** switching noise suppression back to DeepFilterNet 3 no longer plays a short click of old
sound, a game's sound is no longer mistaken for a chat app for a moment (which paused Automatic
ChatMix), and the **Strength** help now says what the slider really does: at 50 % the voice ends up
about twice as loud as the game, at 100 % about three times.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
Then click **Update** when the app says the effect in Windows is out of date. Until then your EQ and
microphone effects keep working as in 0.3.0; the DeepFilterNet 3 fix comes with that update.
