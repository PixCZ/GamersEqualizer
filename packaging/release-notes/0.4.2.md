## Your voice, one control

With the voice leveler on, one slider now decides how loud others hear you: its target. The mic
volume in Windows is only how sensitive the mic is, and the app keeps it sensible on its own.

- **One loudness for your voice.** The mic slider in the mixer and in the tray panel sets the voice
  leveler's target. If you had the mic below 100 % in the mixer, you now sound louder by that much:
  turn the target down.
- **Teams and Zoom stop fighting the leveler.** When a call app keeps moving the mic volume itself,
  the leveler holds your loudness until the call ends and then puts the mic volume back. Apps that do
  it in two calls are listed on the Mic page, with the option to switch off in them.
- **Mic sensitivity that follows your mic.** It is lowered when the mic clips and raised when your
  voice is too weak, whatever loudness you picked, and it stops when an app keeps setting it back.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
Then update the effect in Windows when the app asks (the tray panel has an **Update** button for it):
until then the microphone runs without its processing.
