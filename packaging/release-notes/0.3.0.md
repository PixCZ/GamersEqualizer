## Hear your squad

New in this release: **Automatic ChatMix**. When someone in voice chat talks over a firefight, the game
steps back. When they stop, it comes back. No hotkey, no slider to ride mid-fight.

It took a wild ride to get here: it started with about 40 settings to tune and ended deliberately
simple. The game is lowered by one amount that it learns from the fights, nothing more. In our
tests it works well, but it needs more players, so **it is off by default**. To try it, switch on **Automatic ChatMix** in **Output & headphones** or on the
**Mixer & device** page.

- **Callouts cut through.** While a teammate talks and the game drowns them out, the game is lowered
  smoothly and comes back up when the talk stops. Nobody talking: nothing changes.
- **You set how much.** One **Strength** slider, 50 % by default. At 50 % the voice ends up about twice
  as loud as the game, at 100 % about four times.
- **The same amount every fight, no pumping.** It starts at zero each time you play, learns from the
  fights how far to go, and eases back so slowly you do not hear it.
- **Your game volume comes back.** Turn the feature off or quit, and the game returns to where you left
  it, even after a crash at the next start.

Works with chat apps on the Chat channel (Discord, TeamSpeak ...); voice chat built into the game is
part of the game's sound and cannot be told apart from it. So far it has been tried mostly with Dolby
Atmos on; without it, it has been listened to much less. Tell us how it behaves in your games: send a
short description and the app log, as in "Reporting problems" in the README.

**Also:** a game's genre is no longer looked up again at almost every launch.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
Then click **Update** when the app says the effect in Windows is out of date: this release needs it,
and until then your EQ and microphone effects pass sound through unchanged. Automatic ChatMix stays
off until you switch it on.
