## Fine-tuning

A small release that polishes what is already there. Nothing new to learn: the echo canceller takes a
shorter path, the microphone level stays steadier, and on laptops with two graphics cards the window
lets the NVIDIA card sleep.

- **The echo canceller takes a shorter path.** It now gets what the speakers play straight from the
  output EQ instead of recording the device on the side, so the service has one stream less open and
  your sidetone never ends up in it.
- **Your voice level holds.** Opening the window during a call no longer resets what the voice
  leveler learned.
- **Laptops can sleep the big graphics card.** The window no longer keeps the NVIDIA card awake, and
  the graphics card choice in Windows settings or the NVIDIA panel applies to it again.

**Also:** clearer warnings, and the Mic tab says why echo cancellation has no speaker signal.

### Removed

Echo cancellation now works only on an output with GamersEqualizer's output EQ installed (which the EQ,
game profiles and sidetone already need). Install it in **Output & headphones**.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
When the app says the effect in Windows is out of date, update it too.
