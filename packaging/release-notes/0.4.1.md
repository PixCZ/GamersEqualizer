## Polished

This release is about the details: the window and the tray panel are easier to read and easy to use, also
late at night, on a small screen or without a mouse. Every page was checked for contrast, wording and
layout, so things look the same everywhere and say plainly what they do.

- **Text you can read at a glance.** Buttons and selected options now have white text on a darker
  orange, and grey text has enough contrast in both themes. Icons show again instead of empty squares.
- **Every control explains itself.** Hover over a setting and a short tooltip says what it does, in
  plain words instead of audio jargon. Tab reaches every control with a visible focus ring.
- **ChatMix and mic loudness in the tray panel.** Set the game / chat balance (or how strongly
  Automatic ChatMix ducks the game) and how loud others hear you, and mute the mic, without opening
  the window.
- **Scrolling no longer edits your EQ.** The mouse wheel changes a band's width only with Alt held, and
  the Frequency, Gain and Width sliders line up.
- **Reset to original.** An imported EQ profile remembers where it came from, and one button in the
  profile bar brings it back.

**Also:** Automatic ChatMix is on for new setups (if you turned it off, it stays off), volume changes
slide instead of jumping, bass bands are exact on 96 and 192 kHz devices, sidetone sounds clean again
after a pause, and the open window uses much less CPU.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
When the app says the effect in Windows is out of date, update it too (the tray panel has an **Update**
button for it): the smoother volume changes and the exact bass bands come with it.
