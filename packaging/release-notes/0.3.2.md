## Follows your devices

A small release of fixes, found on a laptop with a headset that comes and goes. The app now goes by
one microphone and one output everywhere, so sidetone, the tray icon and the warnings agree with
what the mixer shows.

- **Sidetone plays after you unplug a headset.** With the microphone you picked unplugged, the app
  showed "Default microphone" and worked with it, but sidetone kept waiting for the unplugged one and
  stayed silent. It now follows the microphone in use.
- **The tray icon turns red on every recording.** On some PCs Windows does not tell that an app
  records, so the icon stayed plain and its bars did not move. It now also goes by the microphone
  effect itself (with microphone processing on).
- **The bars start where your voice is.** When a recording starts, the icon no longer shows full
  bars for a second before it drops to the real level.

**Also:** the warning about a microphone that is not the Windows default appears only for a
microphone you picked yourself, and the remembered voice level and echo delay belong to the
microphone in use.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
When the app says the effect in Windows is out of date, update it too. If your Windows default
output is a virtual device (Sonar, VB-Cable), switch sidetone on once more: it now belongs to your
real headphones.
