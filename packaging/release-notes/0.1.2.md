## Better profiles

**Every game gets a fitting profile.** A game without its own profile now gets one for its genre
(shooter, racing, MOBA / strategy, ...), taken from GOG Galaxy's labels or the Steam store tags.
Steam tools such as Wallpaper Engine or SteamVR no longer count as the game you play, and Microsoft
Store apps count as games only when they are installed as games (XboxGames folder).

**Take your settings with you.** Settings > **Export settings...** saves your profiles and settings
as one `.json.gz` file, for example before reinstalling Windows; **Import settings...** loads it on
another PC. You choose what goes in and what comes back out, for example only your game profiles to
give to a friend. Devices are matched by name, so profiles land on the right headphones and microphone
even though Windows gives them other ids on the new PC.

**SteelSeries Sonar presets, even without Sonar.** The first-run setup offers to import your Sonar
profiles. If SteelSeries GG is installed but Sonar never ran, its built-in presets are read straight
from GG. New presets from a GG update appear by themselves; profiles you deleted or renamed stay
that way. GG's files are only read, never changed.

**Apps stay on the right channel.** One rule now sorts apps for the mixer and the stream EQ:

- browsers always play on Media, so YouTube keeps full quality even after a Meet call;
- chat apps (Discord, TeamSpeak, Teams, Signal, Telegram, WhatsApp, ...) go to Chat;
- only games the app recognizes go to Game, so Steam itself or a launcher no longer does;
- everything else plays on Media. Drag an app in the mixer and it stays there.

**The microphone level settles faster.** The voice leveler starts from the level it learned last
time and keeps it through a loud first sentence or a cough. If your voice is much quieter or louder
today, it adjusts after the first second of speech instead of creeping there over minutes. Once you
have talked for a while, breathing no longer moves the volume.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
