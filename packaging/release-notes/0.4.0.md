## One click away

Most of what you change mid-game no longer needs the window. Left-click the tray icon and a small
panel opens right there, with your apps, your channels and your output. It also tells you when
something needs fixing, and fixes it.

- **Per-app volume from the tray.** Every app that plays sound has its own slider, with its real
  icon, and the level is remembered for that app next time.
- **Your channels and your output at hand.** Game, Chat, Media and Mic sit beside the apps, and you
  can switch the output device from the same panel.
- **Setup problems come with their fix.** The panel names the first problem in one line, with a
  button that fixes it; for sound effects that need an update, it starts the update itself.
- **Your choice when Windows moves your output.** The panel offers **Switch back** and also
  **Keep ...**, for when you use the new output on purpose.
- **Updates no longer warn about devices that cannot play.** An output Windows cannot open (such as
  a digital output with nothing plugged in) is left alone without a warning, and the update still
  finishes when every device is skipped.

**Also:** the window's notice about a changed output now reads above its two buttons.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
When the app says the effect in Windows is out of date, update it too (the tray panel has an **Update**
button for it).
