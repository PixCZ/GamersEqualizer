## Rock solid

This release is about one thing: GamersEqualizer holds up when something goes wrong. A failed
update, a headset pulled out mid-game or quitting at the wrong moment no longer costs you your sound
or your settings. Most of the code was rebuilt and rechecked along the way, and it runs as fast and
sounds the same as before.

- **Your sound survives a failed update.** Your devices keep the version that worked.
- **Your settings survive surprises.** A Sonar import no longer overwrites a profile you changed,
  and edits made right before you quit or uninstall are saved.
- **Your devices can come and go.** Unplug the headset or plug in a monitor: the Master volume and
  the echo canceller stay on the right device.
- **Your microphone starts clean.** After a pause, the first word no longer carries a scrap of old
  sound.

**Also:** "(none)" for a detected game now really plays it without a profile, you can type into the
profile filter again, and the voice leveler keeps what it learned after **Learn again**.

### Removed

The processed microphone is no longer copied to VB-Cable or "Sonar - Microphone". If an app records
from there, pick your real microphone in that app.

### Updating

Unpack the ZIP, start `gamersequalizer.exe` and click **Update**. Your settings and profiles stay.
